LEGAL SERVICES

Support for life's
important matters.

TGB Law provides legal services in selected areas of practice in Trinidad and Tobago. The scope of assistance will depend on the facts and requirements of each matter.

01

Family Law

Legal assistance in family-related matters, including selected issues concerning children, maintenance, custody and access, and related applications.

02

Property & Real Estate

Guidance on property-related legal matters, including title considerations, ownership matters, agreements and related documentation.

03

Probate & Estate Planning

Assistance with wills, probate applications, grants of representation, resealing applications and estate administration.

04

Conveyancing

Legal support for property transactions, including review and preparation of transaction documents and guidance through the conveyancing process.

05

Legal Document Preparation

Preparation of selected legal documents, declarations, powers of attorney and other documents, subject to instructions and applicable requirements.

06

Paralegal Services

Selected paralegal and legal support services, depending on the nature of the assignment and any applicable professional requirements.
