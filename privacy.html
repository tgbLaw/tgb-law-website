PRIVACY

Privacy & website use.

This notice explains what information may be collected when you use this website and how information submitted through the enquiry form is handled.

Website enquiries

Please do not submit confidential or sensitive information through this website unless TGB Law has confirmed a secure method for doing so and agreed to receive it.

Information collected

The Contact page includes an online enquiry form. If you submit it, the information you provide (including your name, email address, telephone number if supplied, area of enquiry and message) is processed through Formspree and transmitted to TGB Law for the purpose of responding to your enquiry. Please do not include confidential, sensitive or time-critical information in the form. The website does not provide a client portal.

How enquiry information is used

Information submitted through the enquiry form is used to review and respond to your enquiry and, where appropriate, to communicate with you about a possible consultation or legal services. Please provide only the general information needed for an initial enquiry. Do not send confidential documents, sensitive personal information or information relating to urgent deadlines through the form.

Third-party services

The enquiry form is provided by Formspree. This website is hosted using GitHub Pages and uses Google Fonts to display its typography. These third-party providers may process information, including technical data, when their services are used. Their handling of information is subject to their respective privacy practices.

Questions about privacy

If you have a question about this notice or information you have submitted through the website, please contact TGB Law at info@tgblawtt.com.
